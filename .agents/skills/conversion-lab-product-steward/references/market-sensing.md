# External-context sensing

Use this procedure when a current external fact could change Conversion Lab’s positioning, priority, integration contract, or delivery feasibility.

## Research lanes

Inspect only the lanes relevant to the decision:

- challenge rules, deadlines, judging criteria, and submission mechanics;
- WebMCP specification, browser support, and host behavior;
- Shopify WebMCP, Catalog, UCP, Admin API, app, permissions, and merchant product changes;
- OpenAI Ads APIs, access, feeds, campaign controls, insights, conversions, rate limits, and changelog;
- direct merchant/agency problem evidence, workflow frequency, alternatives, willingness to connect data, and willingness to pay;
- competitor or substitute capability that overlaps the exact customer job;
- legal, privacy, platform-policy, or security constraints on product data and external effects.

## Source order

Prefer:

1. official rules, specifications, API documentation, and changelogs;
2. authoritative provider product announcements;
3. direct customer interviews, support requests, usage, and outcome data;
4. primary company filings or published datasets;
5. independent research with disclosed methods;
6. competitor marketing only as evidence of its own claims, not market truth.

Open and inspect the source. Do not rely on search snippets. For unstable facts, record the observation date and link. Label first-party performance claims as directional unless independently verified.

## Evidence record

For each consequential observation capture:

```text
Observation:
Source and publication/update date:
Observed on:
Authority and limitations:
Confidence:
Roadmap claim affected:
Product implication:
Expiry or next recheck:
```

Keep observation separate from inference. Absence of search results is not evidence of absent demand or capability.

## Shift thresholds

- **Immediate replan:** official deadline/rule change, API removal, access loss, breaking schema/permission change, or a safety/legal constraint that blocks the current path.
- **Milestone adjustment:** one authoritative platform capability materially changes feasibility or duplication; validate impact with a focused spike.
- **Product hypothesis review:** repeated direct customer evidence or observed outcome data contradicts the job, buyer, or value mechanism.
- **Material pivot:** require either direct customer/outcome evidence across multiple cases or an authoritative platform change that invalidates the current value proposition. Record the decision explicitly.

Prefer the cheapest reversible response: contract spike, merchant interview, catalogue audit, usability session, shadow comparison, or limited adapter proof. Do not answer uncertainty with an unbounded feature build.

## Cadence

- During the challenge: recheck official requirements once before feature freeze and once before submission; recheck integration docs immediately before implementation.
- Standalone beta: monthly platform scan plus evidence review at each milestone gate.
- Mature product: scheduled market/platform review tied to roadmap decisions, with alerts for official changelogs where available.

This review is advisory and should not run as a nondeterministic merge gate.
